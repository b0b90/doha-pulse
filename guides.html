Doha Metro guide

The Doha Metro runs on three lines — Red, Green, and Gold — connecting Hamad International Airport, West Bay, Lusail, Katara, Education City, and more.

How to ride

Use a Karwa smartcard or the app for fares. The Red Line is the one most visitors use: airport to West Bay to Lusail, with the Corniche and Souq Waqif on the way.

  • Red Line: Airport · West Bay · Lusail
  • Green Line: Education City · Msheireb
  • Gold Line: Old Doha · Katara

Check the official metro map before you travel. Trains are frequent, air-conditioned, and the fastest way across the city at rush hour.

Best parks in Doha

Look for shade, water, play areas, and metro access. Aspire Park, MIA Park, and Katara are good starting points for families.

Arrive early in summer, and late afternoon in winter. MIA Park at sunset — with the skyline across the water — is one of the best free evenings in the city.

First time in Doha?

Start at Souq Waqif in the evening, walk the Corniche at sunset, visit the Museum of Islamic Art, then take the metro to Katara or Lusail. One day, four places, no car needed.
