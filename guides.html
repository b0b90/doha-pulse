Doha Metro guide

The Doha Metro runs on three lines — Red, Green, and Gold — connecting Hamad International Airport, West Bay, Lusail, Katara, Education City, and more. Check the official metro map before you travel, and use a Karwa smartcard or the app for fares.

Best parks in Doha

Look for shade, water, play areas, and metro access. Aspire Park, MIA Park, and Katara are good starting points for families. Arrive early in summer, and late afternoon in winter.

First time in Doha?

Start at Souq Waqif in the evening, walk the Corniche at sunset, visit the Museum of Islamic Art, then take the metro to Katara or Lusail. One day, four places, no car needed.
